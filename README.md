# AI Evals in Practice: For Engineers & PMs — Free Preview

Learn to build evaluation frameworks you can trust, then use them to ship AI products that survive production.

**Instructor:** Manjeet Singh · **Full course:** [AI Evals in Practice](https://live.bytebytego.com/courses/ai-evals) (ByteByteGo Live, 6 live sessions)

This repo is the free preview of the paid course. It gives you a real taste, not a brochure: one complete hands-on lab, two browser tools, the full syllabus, study flashcards, and one live session deck. The full course adds the rest: 12 assignments, worked solutions, datasets, and the live sessions themselves.

## Try it now, no setup

**[Open the hands-on lab in your browser](https://coachmanjeet.github.io/AI-Agent-Evals-Course-Preview/hands-on-practice/)**

You are the product manager for Pronto, a demo grocery delivery agent. Three tabs: write your first eval, trace your first failure, and make the ship or hold call across four weekly scorecards. Nothing to install, no keys, no account. Or download the `hands-on-practice/` folder and double-click `index.html`.

## The 6 weeks

See [`syllabus.md`](syllabus.md) for the full map. In short: Week 1 eval foundations and error analysis, Week 2 LLM judges, Week 3 safety and adversarial testing, Week 4 RAG evaluation, Week 5 multi-agent evaluation, Week 6 production eval infrastructure. One demo agent, Pronto, runs through all six weeks.
